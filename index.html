Inspired by Shannon's Strength

Standing together. Playing for a purpose.

What began as a community rallying around Shannon during her battle with a rare form of breast cancer has grown into an annual tradition.

The Shan Strong Charity Golf Tournament brings together friends, family, coworkers, golfers, and local businesses to celebrate resilience, support one another, and create hope for others affected by breast cancer.

Together, we continue to stand Shan Strong.

A meaningful day on the course.
